Frequently asked questions

Do you travel to me?

Yes. Travel is free within a 15 km radius of Namur. Beyond that an extra travel fee applies, so contact us for a personalised quote.

How do I book?

Call +32 484 83 21 66, message us on WhatsApp or Facebook, email info.cm.ride@gmail.com, or use the form on this page.

Is the price the same for every car?

The advertised price is for a city car. A sedan adds €10 and an SUV adds €20.

What if my car is very dirty?

Very dirty vehicles, heated steam-water cleaning at 140°C and other special requests are priced by quote. Send us a message with a photo and we will come back with a price.

Can you clean two cars at once?

Yes. The Duo package covers interior and exterior for two vehicles at €120.
